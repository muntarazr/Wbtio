Why you can trust the numbers

Every figure is calculated in code from your data. The AI never does arithmetic. It receives the finished numbers and writes the commentary around them — and the email reads the figures back from the calculation, not from the AI's text. Language models are unreliable at maths, and one wrong number would undermine every number after it. This design removes that risk rather than reducing it.

What it will never do

  • Estimate, round, or invent a figure — every number traces to a row in your data.
  • Show "infinite growth" when last week was zero. It says no comparison is available.
  • Silently drop unreadable rows. They are counted and disclosed at the foot of the report.
  • Manufacture a concern to look thorough. A good week is reported as a good week.
